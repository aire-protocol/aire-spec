# AIRE Governance

## Status

AIRE is currently led by a BDFL (Benevolent Dictator For Life): the project founder.

This is intentional. Protocols designed by committee tend to ship slowly and badly. The goal is to get to a working v1.0 fast, prove adoption, then transition to neutral governance.

## Path to neutral governance

- **v0.x — BDFL.** Single decision-maker. Fast iteration. Public spec, open to PRs and issues.
- **v1.0 — Steering committee.** Add 2–4 implementers who have shipped real AIRE code in production.
- **Post-1.0 — Foundation donation.** Donate the spec to a neutral body (Linux Foundation or IETF). Reference implementations remain under their respective maintainers.

## Decision-making

For v0.x:

- BDFL has final say on spec changes.
- Pull requests are reviewed and merged by BDFL or designated reviewers.
- Major decisions (frame format, identity model) are documented in the spec with rationale.

## Contribution

Contributions are welcome via pull request. By contributing, you agree to license your contribution under Apache 2.0, including the patent grant.

## Patent policy

AIRE is licensed under Apache 2.0, which includes an explicit patent grant from contributors to all users of the spec and reference implementations. Contributors must not knowingly contribute material covered by patents they cannot license under Apache 2.0 terms.

## Code of conduct

Be excellent. No harassment, no bad-faith argumentation, no vendor games.
